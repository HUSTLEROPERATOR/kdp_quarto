# AI Writing Prompts – Quarto Book Template

Ready-to-use prompts for generating chapter drafts, back-cover copy, and
marketing content with AI tools (Claude, GPT-4, or similar).

Replace all `[PLACEHOLDER]` text with your book-specific details before using.

---

## System Prompt (use at the start of every AI session)

```
You are a professional ghostwriter specialising in [GENRE] non-fiction.
You are writing for [AUTHOR NAME], who is an expert in [FIELD/TOPIC].

STYLE RULES:
- Write in [LANGUAGE], with a [warm/direct/technical/narrative] tone
- [AUTHOR] speaks in the first person
- Avoid academic or promotional language
- Use varied sentence length for a natural rhythm
- Prefer concrete stories and examples over abstract statements
- Technical sections must be accessible to a general reader
- Target length: [TARGET WORD COUNT] words per chapter

AUTHOR PERSONA:
- [Describe the author's personality, expertise, and voice]
- [Key values or beliefs the author holds]
- [What makes this author unique]
```

## Chapter Draft Prompt

```
Write Chapter [NUMBER] of the book "[BOOK TITLE]".

TITLE: [CHAPTER TITLE]
OBJECTIVE: [What the reader will learn or experience in this chapter]

REQUIRED STRUCTURE:
1. Opening narrative or anecdote ([300–400] words): [Describe the opening scene or hook]
2. Main section 1 ([X] words): [Topic and key points]
3. Main section 2 ([X] words): [Topic and key points]
4. Main section 3 ([X] words): [Topic and key points]
5. Closing reflection ([300] words): [Summarise the lesson or leave the reader with a question]

TONE: [e.g. educational but warm / literary but accessible / practical and direct]

AVOID: [List things to avoid, e.g. clichés, jargon, etc.]

INCLUDE: [Any tables, lists, or special elements needed]
```

## Book Blurb / Back-Cover Copy Prompt

```
Write a back-cover blurb for the book "[BOOK TITLE]" by [AUTHOR NAME].

BOOK OVERVIEW:
[2–3 sentences describing what the book is about]

TARGET READER:
[Describe the ideal reader: who they are, what problem they have, what they want]

TONE: [e.g. inspiring / practical / urgent / warm]

FORMAT:
- Opening hook (1 sentence): grab attention immediately
- Problem statement (2–3 sentences): the pain or challenge the reader faces
- Solution teaser (2–3 sentences): what this book offers, without giving everything away
- Author credibility (1–2 sentences): why [AUTHOR] is the right person to write this
- Call to action (1 sentence): invite the reader to open the book

LENGTH: 150–200 words maximum.
```

## Chapter Summary Prompt (for Table of Contents or marketing)

```
Write a one-paragraph summary of Chapter [NUMBER]: "[CHAPTER TITLE]" from the book "[BOOK TITLE]".

The summary should:
- Be 60–80 words long
- Tell the reader what they will learn in this chapter
- Sound exciting and useful, not like an academic abstract
- Match the overall tone of the book: [TONE]
```

## Social Media Post Prompt

```
Write [NUMBER] social media posts based on the key ideas from Chapter [NUMBER] of "[BOOK TITLE]".

KEY IDEAS FROM THE CHAPTER:
[Paste 3–5 bullet points with the main insights]

PLATFORM: [Instagram / LinkedIn / X (Twitter)]
TONE: [e.g. inspiring / informative / conversational]
LENGTH: [e.g. 150 words max for LinkedIn / 280 characters for X]
HASHTAGS: Include [NUMBER] relevant hashtags at the end.

Each post should stand alone and not require the reader to have read the book.
```

## Newsletter / Email Announcement Prompt

```
Write a launch announcement email for the book "[BOOK TITLE]" by [AUTHOR NAME].

AUDIENCE: My email list of [describe your audience]
LAUNCH DATE: [DATE]
WHERE TO BUY: [Amazon KDP link / website]
PRICE: [PRICE]

EMAIL STRUCTURE:
- Subject line (5–8 words, creates curiosity or urgency)
- Opening: personal note from the author (2–3 sentences)
- What the book is about (2–3 sentences)
- Who it is for (2–3 sentences)
- What the reader will get out of it (bullet list, 3–5 points)
- Call to action: buy / download / share
- Closing: warm sign-off from the author

TONE: [e.g. warm and personal / excited and direct]
LENGTH: 250–350 words (not counting the subject line)
```

## Interview-to-Prose Prompt

Use this when you have raw interview notes or transcripts to turn into book-ready prose.

```
Transform this interview excerpt into polished narrative prose suitable for Chapter [NUMBER] of "[BOOK TITLE]".

ORIGINAL INTERVIEW EXCERPT:
[Paste the raw transcript or notes here]

RULES:
- Keep the author's original words and meaning intact
- Convert spoken language into readable prose without losing authenticity
- Integrate the passage naturally into the chapter context
- If any statement seems unclear or needs verification, mark it with [VERIFY WITH AUTHOR]
- Target length: approximately [X] words

STYLE: First person, [warm/direct/reflective] tone consistent with the rest of the book.
```

---

## Tips for Better AI Outputs

1. **Be specific:** The more context you give, the better the output.
2. **Iterate:** The first draft is always a starting point, not a final product.
3. **Flag inventions:** AI may invent facts. Always mark uncertain claims with `[VERIFY]`.
4. **Check technical content:** Numbers, dates, recipes, and procedures must be verified independently.
5. **Your voice is the goal:** Rewrite until the text sounds like you, not like an AI.
