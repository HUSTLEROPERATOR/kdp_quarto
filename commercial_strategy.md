|---|---|---|---|
| Paperback | $ | 60% | $ |
| Hardcover | $ | 60% | $ |

**KDP royalty tiers:**
- Kindle: 70% royalty on books priced $2.99–$9.99; 35% outside that range.
- Paperback: 60% royalty minus printing cost. Use the [KDP royalty calculator](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G201834340).

## Content Flywheel

Books are more profitable when each chapter generates additional content.

```
BOOK CHAPTER
↓
Blog post / newsletter article
↓
Social media carousel (Instagram / LinkedIn)
↓
Short video / YouTube
↓
Workshop or online course module
```

Map your chapters to the flywheel:

| Chapter | Blog post? | Social post? | Video? | Workshop? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Introduction | ☐ | ☐ | ☐ | ☐ |
| Chapter 1 | ☐ | ☐ | ☐ | ☐ |
| Chapter 2 | ☐ | ☐ | ☐ | ☐ |

## Launch Plan

### Pre-launch

- [ ] Set up author website or landing page
- [ ] Build email list (target: __ subscribers before launch)
- [ ] Reach out to potential reviewers / ARC readers
- [ ] Set up social profiles

### Launch week

- [ ] Publish on KDP (allow 24–72 h for approval)
- [ ] Email list announcement
- [ ] Social media campaign
- [ ] Request honest reviews from ARC readers
- [ ] Consider Kindle Countdown Deal or KDP Select Free Days

### Post-launch

- [ ] Monitor and respond to reviews
- [ ] Refresh Amazon description / A+ content
- [ ] Pitch to relevant blogs, podcasts, newsletters
- [ ] Begin Phase 2 product development
